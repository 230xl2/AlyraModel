# Alyra Model

### Exploring the brain. Understanding Alzheimer's. Making neuroscience more accessible.

**Alyra Model** is an AI-assisted neuroscience exploration platform focused on Alzheimer's disease, brain biology, and the mechanisms involved in neurodegeneration.

The project brings together interactive 3D brain visualization, educational research content, biological pathway exploration, and AI-assisted explanations to make complex neuroscience topics easier to explore and understand.

## Overview

Neuroscience involves complex biological systems, interconnected pathways, and mechanisms that can be difficult to visualize.

Alyra Model aims to make these concepts more accessible through an interactive environment where users can explore brain regions, investigate disease-related mechanisms, and learn about the science behind Alzheimer's disease.

Rather than presenting neuroscience as static information, Alyra provides tools for visual exploration and structured learning.

## Features

### 1. Interactive 3D Brain Explorer

Explore a 3D representation of the human brain and investigate different anatomical regions.

- Navigate brain structures.
- Explore individual brain regions.
- View educational information about anatomy and function.
- Understand how different regions relate to broader neurological processes.

### 2. Alzheimer's Disease Mechanisms

Explore biological mechanisms associated with Alzheimer's disease, including:

- **Amyloid accumulation (APP):** Learn about amyloid precursor protein processing and the formation of amyloid-beta.
- **Tau dysregulation (MAPT):** Explore tau protein dysfunction and its relationship to neuronal health.
- **Neuroinflammation (TREM2):** Understand the role of microglia and immune-related pathways in the brain.
- **Oxidative stress (SOD1):** Examine oxidative stress and its potential effects on cellular function.

These topics provide an educational introduction to important areas of Alzheimer's research.

### 3. AI-Assisted Research Exploration

Alyra incorporates AI-assisted explanations to help users navigate complex neuroscience topics.

The AI Notebook provides an environment for exploring biological concepts and research-related information, including public research references.

AI-generated explanations are intended to support learning and exploration. They should not be treated as definitive scientific conclusions.

### 4. Research Data Integration

The platform incorporates information from public research resources to provide additional context.

Current integrations include:

- **Open Targets:** Gene-disease association information, including Alzheimer's-related associations.
- **Broad Institute DepMap:** Public cancer dependency and CRISPR screening data.
- **National Cancer Institute (NCI):** Cancer-related mutation information.

These sources cover different research domains. DepMap and NCI datasets are cancer research resources and should not be interpreted as Alzheimer's-specific evidence.

### 5. Educational Exploration

Alyra is designed to make neuroscience approachable for different levels of experience.

The platform focuses on helping users understand:

- Brain anatomy and regional functions.
- Alzheimer's disease biology.
- Molecular mechanisms.
- Neuroinflammation.
- Current research directions.
- The relationship between genes, proteins, and neurological processes.

## Scientific Approach

Alyra Model is an educational and exploratory project.

The platform combines established scientific concepts, public research resources, interactive visualizations, and AI-assisted explanations.

An important distinction is maintained between:

- Published research findings.
- Publicly available biological datasets.
- AI-generated explanations.
- Illustrative simulations and model-generated values.

Some interactive visualizations use illustrative values to demonstrate biological concepts. These are not patient measurements, clinical results, or experimentally validated predictions.

The project does not claim to diagnose, predict, prevent, or treat Alzheimer's disease.
